**Version:** v1.0.3

## About

**R2T1A Riichi Score Lab** is a free, multilingual Riichi Mahjong score calculator designed to make scoring easier to understand and use. It helps players calculate **Han (翻), Fu (符), total points, and payments from other players** under standard four-player Japanese Riichi Mahjong rules.

## Features

### Fast Calculation

- Enter Han and Fu directly to estimate a winning hand's score.
- Use the meld-based Fu calculator when needed.
- View Ron and Tsumo payments for dealer and non-dealer hands.
- Include Honba counters and Riichi sticks.

### Regular Calculation

- Build a hand by selecting Mahjong tiles.
- Add optional melds, including Chi, Pon, Open Kan, and Closed Kan.
- Set the winning tile, Riichi status, seat wind, round wind, and winning method.
- Enter Dora indicators, Ura-Dora indicators, and red fives.
- View detected Yaku, Han, Fu, points, and player payment details.
- Switch between Classic Tiles and Real Tiles.

### Languages

- English
- Simplified Chinese (简体中文)
- Japanese (日本語)

## How to Use

1. Open the [official website](https://R2T1AEvern1ght.github.io/R2T1A-Riichi-Score-Lab/).
2. Choose **Fast Calculation** or **Regular Calculation**.
3. Enter the requested hand and round information. In Regular Calculation, follow the numbered steps; Step 0 is optional.
4. Review the automatically updated scoring results.

## Important Notice

This is an independent, fan-made calculator. Although it supports many common scoring situations, unusual Yaku, Yakuman combinations, and special rules may require manual verification. Please report any errors you find.
